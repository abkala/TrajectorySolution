01 / NCS REFERENCE

Cloud & Virtualisation

NCS describes hybrid infrastructure that places workloads across cloud environments according to performance, risk and cost requirements, with supporting connectivity, security and operations.

Cloud & Virtualisation: 5 capability notes
Hybrid Cloud / Hyper Converged Infra
Private and public infrastructure are deployed and managed around an organisation's security, compliance and performance needs.
Virtualisation / Containerisation
Virtual machines and containers support workload modernisation, more flexible scaling and better use of computing resources.
Cloud Connectivity
Connections link cloud environments with data centres and branch locations, addressing reliability, speed and protection in transit.
Cloud Security
Layered controls cover identities, data, networks and applications as part of protecting cloud environments and supporting compliance.
Cloud & Infrastructure Managed Services
NCS lists ongoing operational management, proactive optimisation and support governed by service-level agreements. Specific terms require confirmation with NCS.

Enterprise AI

NCS connects AI adoption with secured computing and storage, while retaining organisational control of data, risk and compliance.

Enterprise AI: 4 capability notes
Private AI Platforms
AI can run within an organisation-controlled environment to address data-sovereignty and regulatory requirements.
AI Factory
A platform approach brings model creation, training and deployment into an enterprise-scale development workflow.
Cyber for AI
Protection addresses models, training datasets and inference pipelines, including adversarial interference, data poisoning and other AI-specific threats.
AI for Cyber & IT Operations
AI is applied to threat detection, incident-response automation and the optimisation of IT operations.

03 / NCS REFERENCE

Workforce Evolution

This NCS area covers the digital workplace: access to working environments, device administration, endpoint protection and support for employees and customers.

Workforce Evolution: 6 capability notes
Digital Workspace
A unified, protected workspace supports collaboration and productive work across locations.
Endpoint Management
Device visibility is paired with automated setup, patching and enforcement of organisational policies.
Endpoint Security
Behaviour-based analysis and automated responses support the detection, investigation and containment of endpoint threats.
Service Desk / Contact Centre
Service-desk and contact-centre systems provide support channels for both employees and customers.
Virtual Desktops & Apps
On-demand access to desktops and applications from different devices reduces reliance on a particular physical workstation.
End User Techbar
A walk-up support model handles technical issues and service requests, with an emphasis on limiting interruptions to employees' work.

04 / NCS REFERENCE

Secured Connectivity

NCS brings network delivery and network protection together across cloud platforms, data centres and distributed enterprise sites.

Secured Connectivity: 6 capability notes
Networking
Enterprise networks are designed, deployed and managed to meet connectivity and performance requirements.
Secure Access Service Edge
A cloud-delivered framework combines networking and security controls for users, devices and applications.
Network Detection & Response
Traffic analysis, behavioural detection and automated alerts help identify threats moving through a network.
WAN Operations
Wide area network management addresses routing, bandwidth allocation and prioritisation according to application needs.
Network Perimeter Security
Boundary protection includes next-generation firewalls, intrusion prevention and filtering of threatening traffic.
Quantum Safe Security
NCS lists cryptographic protection intended to prepare enterprise communications and data for threats from quantum computing.

Integrated SecOps

NCS groups security monitoring, intelligence, automation and response into a coordinated operational approach.

Integrated SecOps: 4 capability notes
Security Information Event Management (SIEM)
Security information from different systems is brought together and analysed to support detection, investigation and response.
Security Orchestration, Automation & Response (SOAR)
Incident workflows are coordinated and automated to reduce repetitive response tasks for security teams.
Threat Intelligence Platforms
Curated threat information helps teams interpret changing risks and decide how to adjust defences.
Agentic SecOps
NCS describes AI agents that assist with threat triage, investigation and response, reducing the amount of manual intervention required.

06 / NCS REFERENCE

Access Management

NCS addresses who and what may reach enterprise resources, combining identity governance, authentication, cryptographic trust and device checks.

Access Management: 7 capability notes
Multi-Factor Authentication
Access requires more than one form of verification to protect critical systems from unauthorised users.
Hardware Security Module
Tamper-resistant hardware protects cryptographic keys and sensitive cryptographic operations.
PKI / Certification Authority
Digital certificates and public key infrastructure establish trusted identities and support encrypted communications and transactions.
Role-Based Access Control
Permissions for network resources and devices are assigned by role, applying the principle of least privilege.
Human Cyber Risk Management
Awareness programmes, behavioural analysis and targeted measures address security risks involving people.
Network Admission Control
Device posture is checked against policy before equipment is admitted to the network.
Identity Access Management
Adaptive identity controls govern enterprise access and adjust to current risk signals.
NCS access management detail ↗
07 / NCS REFERENCE

Cyber Resilience

NCS links cybersecurity preparation with the ability to restore systems, recover data and sustain business operations after disruption.

Cyber Resilience: 5 capability notes
Disaster Recovery & Business Continuity
Tested recovery plans address restoration of essential systems and data, with attention to downtime and business impact.
Threat Modelling & Incident Response
Attack-path analysis and simulations build readiness; response playbooks and post-incident reviews support incident handling and learning.
Data Protect & Backup
Backup, replication and recovery services address data loss and ransomware-related disruption.
Vulnerability Assessment & Penetration Testing
Structured testing, including realistic attack simulations, identifies weaknesses in applications and infrastructure.
Security Risk Management
Framework-based assessment identifies and ranks cyber risks so security priorities can be tied to business objectives.

Service Driven: 4 capability notes

Automation Platform

ITSM / ITAM / ITOM
