01 / CREATE USEFUL EXPERIENCES

Digital product & experience

For people interested in connecting user needs, clear interfaces, and maintainable software.

Explore the digital product path

The work to understand

Translate an ambiguous problem into a user journey, a prototype, and small increments that can be tested. Designers and engineers would work together to make usability and accessibility part of implementation rather than a handoff.

Skills to practise

  • Interviewing without leading the participant toward a preferred answer.
  • Semantic HTML, responsive layouts, and keyboard-accessible interactions.
  • Writing acceptance criteria and explaining technical trade-offs.

A learning project

Redesign a fictional appointment-booking journey. Document the assumptions, build a local prototype, and test cancellation and error recovery as carefully as the happy path. Use synthetic information rather than real personal data.

Possible growth

Explore deeper expertise in service design, front-end engineering, or product discovery. Broader responsibility means improving how a team makes decisions, not just producing more screens.

02 / BUILD DEPENDABLE FOUNDATIONS

Cloud & platform engineering

For people who enjoy understanding how systems fit together and making them easier to operate.

Explore the platform engineering path

The work to understand

Design repeatable environments, delivery pipelines, and operational feedback. A platform is useful when it helps other people ship changes safely and understand what is happening when something fails.

Skills to practise

  • Networking, identity, configuration, and least-privilege access.
  • Versioned infrastructure and automated checks with clear failure messages.
  • Observability, recovery exercises, and cost-aware design.

A learning project

Run a small application locally, add a health check, and rehearse restoring its data from a test backup. Write a runbook another person can follow. If exploring paid cloud services, agree a budget and cleanup plan first.

Possible growth

Develop towards platform design, reliability engineering, or operational security. Look for ways to reduce repeated manual work without hiding important decisions from the people responsible.

03 / REASON FROM EVIDENCE

Data & responsible AI

For people who want to turn information into useful analysis while being honest about its limits.

Explore the data and AI path

The work to understand

Trace where data comes from, examine its quality, and design experiments that distinguish real improvement from an attractive result. Communicating uncertainty is part of the craft, not a footnote.

Skills to practise

  • Data modelling, query design, and reproducible analysis.
  • Recognising missingness, leakage, sampling bias, and misleading comparisons.
  • Evaluating AI output with domain review, privacy boundaries, and simple baselines.

A learning project

Use a synthetic service dataset to investigate a change in demand. Compare a simple rule with a more complex approach. Write down where each fails, what information is missing, and what evidence would change your conclusion.

Possible growth

Explore analytics engineering, data governance, or AI evaluation. Good judgement includes recognising when a simpler tool is easier to trust and maintain.

PRACTICE, NOT JUST POTENTIAL

A learning loop worth keeping.

  1. 01

    Choose a small question

    Set a specific learning goal and agree how you will know you understand it. A working explanation can be more useful than a long list of completed courses.

  2. 02

    Make your thinking visible

    Pair on a problem, request a focused review, and keep notes on the trade-offs. Ask for feedback on the reasoning as well as the result.

  3. 03

    Share what changed

    Show what did not work and why. Turn one lesson into a reusable example, a better checklist, or a clearer question for the next experiment.
