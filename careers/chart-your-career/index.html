DEVELOPMENT WITH A DIRECTION

Connecting career paths
with the skills to grow.

NCS describes career development through a Career Progression Model. Its purpose is to connect possible career directions with role expectations, identify areas for development and guide relevant learning.

The source presents four connected parts: career tracks, a map of roles and skills, competencies, and training or certification. The sections below explain each part in original wording.

01 / DIRECTION

Career track

NCS describes more than one route through a career, including advancement within a track and movement into another area of work.

Explore career pathways
  • Vertical progression offers a way to advance along a career pathway.
  • Lateral movement creates opportunities to explore different work and broaden skills.

The published overview describes these options without specifying promotion thresholds or individual eligibility.

02 / ROLE CLARITY

Job roles and skills map

A role map connects the work expected in a position with the expertise needed to carry it out.

Explore roles and skills
  • Understand the main tasks and responsibilities associated with a role.
  • Identify the expertise needed when considering development or progression.

This reference summarises the purpose of the map; the source page does not publish a full catalogue of NCS job-role specifications.

03 / CAPABILITY

Skills and competencies

NCS groups role development around technical knowledge, functional skills and behavioural competencies.

Explore competency areas
  • Technical skills relate to the technology and specialist knowledge used in the role.
  • Functional skills support the work of the relevant business or professional discipline.
  • Behavioural competencies concern how a person approaches and carries out their work.

These are the three areas identified in the overview, not a published assessment rubric or scoring system.

04 / LEARNING

Training & certification

The model links a role's core skill needs with relevant learning and certification requirements.

Explore training and certification
  • Identify the core capabilities needed for the role.
  • Use those needs to understand which training or credentials are relevant to development.

Specific courses, certification lists, schedules and programme conditions are not provided on the source overview. Check the official NCS resources for current information.
